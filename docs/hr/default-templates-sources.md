# Malaysia HR onboarding and offboarding: official sources and statutory timings

This is the research behind the Malaysian default onboarding and offboarding templates
(`csa/hr/default_templates.py`, reviewed through
[DEFAULT_TEMPLATES_REVIEW.md](./DEFAULT_TEMPLATES_REVIEW.md)). It records facts and citations
from official sources, checked on 2026-11-18; it is not legal advice. Every default step's source
note points at a row of this file. Contribution rates, wage ceilings, headcount thresholds,
benefit rates and penalty amounts are deliberately not recorded here or in the templates: a step
is a reminder with a reference, never a calculation.

## How to read the confidence column

- **Official (read)**: the official page or official statute text was read directly (WebFetch or downloaded PDF). Wording is quoted from it.
- **Official (excerpt)**: the official page could not be fetched directly (HTTP 403 or certificate error); only excerpts of it returned by a search tool were seen. Treat quoted wording as likely but re-verify in a browser.
- **Secondary only**: no official page confirmed it.
- **Unclear**: official sources conflict, are silent, or are out of date.

| n. EA 1955 coverage | Coverage no longer depends on wages for the Act as a whole: all private-sector employees under a contract of service are covered, from 1 January 2023, in Peninsular Malaysia and Labuan.
