# Payments: DuitNow QR, bank transfer and cash, automated for thousands of customers

Status: design for the owner's approval, 2026-11-11. Two pull requests of the business surfaces programme
(`2026-11-10-business-surfaces-programme.md`): **Payments 1** (invoices, payment methods, reminders, the bot and the
console) and **Payments 2** (bank statement import with automatic matching, cash at a meeting through Bookings).
Migrations 0590 to 0599. Depends on customer onboarding (`2026-11-11-customer-onboarding-design.md`).

## 1. Intent

The owner's clients are paid by DuitNow QR, bank transfer or cash (cash means a face-to-face meeting, booked in
Bookings). Today the platform has no payment code. Matching payments by hand works for tens of customers, not for
thousands, so the design makes staff handle **exceptions only**: every invoice carries a reference, payments are
matched to invoices automatically from the company's bank statement, and reminders go out on their own.

The platform never holds money. Each company is its own merchant; the platform records, reminds, matches and reports.

## 2. What exists today (survey of 4d4d808)

- No payment provider, gateway, webhook or proof-upload code. The growth-program design says payments are wanted
  later and tells builders to leave hooks only.
- `orders.payment_status` (unpaid, cod_pending, paid, refunded, failed). The bot's orders are Telegram-only and
  cash on delivery.
- An `invoices` table (id, contact, order, status without a check, amount, currency, issued/due/paid times,
  `payment_ref`, line items) that nothing writes except seeds; a read-only `InvoiceAgent` that sends a signed
  download link (the console route for it returns 501); staff-only invoice preparation that is "not executed".
- Subscriptions carry price, interval and renewal dates; a renewal job exists. WhatsApp sends plain text only and
  proactive WhatsApp messages need approved templates, which are a non-goal of the WhatsApp phase 1.
- Bookings are built (types, people, hours, bot booking, Google Calendar).

## 3. Goals, non-goals, anti-goals

Goals

- Every payable thing (a subscription period, an order) becomes an **invoice** with a short unique **reference**
  and a clear amount; invoices are generated by a job, not by staff.
- The bot gives the customer the company's payment instructions (QR image, bank account, reference, amount) from
  structured settings, answers "did you receive my payment?" from facts, and never says a payment arrived unless one
  is matched.
- Payment is recorded automatically by **statement matching**: staff import the bank statement file (or schedule a
  drop); the matcher pays invoices on exact reference and amount; staff see only the exceptions.
- **Cash** works through Bookings: the bot offers a meeting, the invoice waits as "cash at meeting", and the staff
  member collects and marks it paid from the booking.
- Reminders before due, on due and after due go out automatically on the customer's channel, with limits.
- The console has a Payments tab: open invoices, needs-attention items, imports and payment settings.

Non-goals

- A payment gateway, card payments, card-on-file or automatic debit (the ledger leaves a `source` seam, no provider
  fields now); WhatsApp reminders that need templates (until the template phase, reminders go on Telegram and inside
  an open WhatsApp conversation); tax invoices and e-invoicing (MyInvois); refunds beyond marking an invoice void;
  reading receipts with a model as proof (the image is stored for staff, never believed).

Anti-goals

- The bot never states that a payment is received, paid or confirmed unless a matched payment exists; a customer's
  receipt image only moves the invoice to "proof submitted".
- No payment amount, account number or reference is ever composed by the model: it comes from facts, and the
  grounding gate (every number must appear in a fact) applies.
- No automatic suspension of a subscription for non-payment unless the company turns it on (default off).
- Bank statement lines are financial personal data: Admin only, audited, the uploaded file is never stored (it is
  parsed on upload and discarded), long digit strings are masked, and the lines are purged after 90 days.

## 4. Approaches

A. **Manual rails with automated reconciliation (recommended).** Matches how the clients are paid today, needs no
   merchant account or provider contract, scales because staff only see exceptions.
B. **Gateway first (FPX or DuitNow through a Malaysian gateway).** Fully automatic confirmation, but each company
   needs a merchant account, fees apply, and it is not how they are paid now. The design keeps a seam so a gateway
   can be added later as a payment `source`.
C. **Bank feed API.** Automatic without files, but availability for small-business accounts in Malaysia is
   unverified; revisit when a bank offers it.

Chosen: A. Payments 2 makes it automatic; Payments 1 works with staff marking paid so the bot flow can ship first.

## 5. Design

Data (migration 0590 onward; additive, tenant isolated, `csa_app` only, partial indexes on open invoices)

- `payment_methods(client_id, kind qr|transfer|cash, label, details jsonb, instructions text, is_active)`: bank name,
  account holder, account number, QR image (object storage key), the reference prefix, and the optional
  unique-amount switch (adds a per-invoice cents suffix when a QR cannot carry a reference; default off).
- `invoices` gains `reference text` (unique per company, short, checksummed), `method kind`, `subscription_id`,
  `period_start/period_end`, a status check (`draft`, `issued`, `proof_submitted`, `paid`, `overdue`, `void`,
  `cash_pending`) and `reminded_at`.
- `payments(client_id, invoice_id nullable, amount, currency, received_at, method kind, source
  statement|staff|cash, statement_line_id nullable, reference_seen, matched_by auto|staff, created_by)` with an
  idempotency key so importing the same line twice never pays twice.
- `invoices.claim_note` (text) holds what the customer says they paid (the bot asks for the time and the bank, as
  text); there is no image upload in these PRs (section 9).
- Payments 2: `statement_imports(client_id, profile, status, imported_by, line_count, purged_at)` and
  `statement_lines(import_id, line_hash unique, posted_at, amount, description, payer_hash, match_status
  matched|suggested|unmatched|ignored, invoice_id nullable)`, plus a saved column-mapping profile per company.

Jobs (the worker; idempotent per invoice and per period; batched with limits)

- **Invoice generator:** for each active subscription whose next period starts within the lead time (default 7 days),
  one invoice per period (unique key subscription, period). Sign-up approval issues the first invoice at once.
- **Reminders:** at lead time, due date, +3 and +7 days overdue (company-configurable list), on the customer's
  channel with a per-contact daily cap, a quiet-hours window, and none for a paid or void invoice. WhatsApp sends only
  inside an open conversation until templates exist.
- **Overdue marker** and, off by default, a suspend rule.

Bot

- `PaymentAgent` (read plus draft): `instructions` (the company's method details, the invoice reference and amount as
  facts), `status` (open invoices of this contact and whether a payment is matched), `claim_paid` (the customer says
  they paid and when: the text is stored as `claim_note` and the invoice moves to `proof_submitted`; the bot says
  "we'll confirm when it shows in our account"),
  `choose_cash` (books a meeting through the Bookings agent and sets the invoice to `cash_pending`). Intent
  `billing_invoice` routes to it; the planner and classifier get rules and examples in English and Malay; handoff
  when the customer disputes an amount.
- Facts carry exact digits so the grounding gate passes; account numbers are facts, never model text.

Console (Payments tab, Admin and staff; settings Admin only)

- Open invoices with filters (overdue, proof submitted, cash pending), a **Needs attention** list (proof without a
  match, unmatched statement lines, amount mismatches, duplicate references), payment settings, and in Payments 2
  the import screen with the match preview, "Match" for a suggested line and "Ignore".
- Staff **Mark paid** (Payments 1) writes a `payments` row with source `staff`, so the ledger is always the truth.
- A paid invoice activates a `pending` subscription (onboarding design) in the same transaction.

Matching rules (Payments 2, in order; first hit wins; every decision is stored with its rule)

1. Reference found in the line's description and amount equals the invoice amount: auto-match, paid.
2. Reference found, amount differs: needs attention (short pay, over pay) with the difference shown.
3. No reference, unique-amount switch on, one open invoice with that exact amount: auto-match.
4. No reference, exactly one open invoice within the amount and a date window whose contact name resembles the payer
   description: suggested (staff confirm in one click, or the company enables auto-accept for suggestions).
5. Otherwise unmatched.

Scale

- Reconciliation touches only open invoices by (client_id, reference) index; a 10,000-line statement is matched in
  batches inside the job, not in the request. Reminders and generation are cursor-based with limits per run, so a
  company with thousands of customers never produces one large transaction. The Needs attention list is the only queue
  staff watch.

## 6. Tests (named for the builder)

- Generator: one invoice per subscription period even when the job runs twice; correct amount and reference;
  stops at cancelled.
- Matcher: each rule above, double import is a no-op, a short pay never marks paid, tenant isolation.
- Reminders: schedule, caps, quiet hours, no reminder for paid or void, channel restriction for WhatsApp.
- Bot: instructions come from facts (digits grounded), "did you receive my payment" never claims paid without a
  matched payment, proof moves the invoice only to `proof_submitted`, cash booking sets `cash_pending`, Malay and
  English golden cases; the semantic review rejects an invented account number.
- Console: invoices list, mark paid, import preview and match, settings, portal twin; Admin-only statement access;
  the raw file is deleted after 30 days.

## 9. Builder check (2026-11-11): calls made by the designer

These settle what the builder check found and supersede any earlier sentence they contradict.

- **No proof images in these PRs.** Telegram photos arrive only as file ids that need each company's bot token to
  fetch, and the worker has no write access to a `payments` storage prefix; adding stores, IAM prefixes and a worker
  grant is infrastructure work out of scope here. The customer states what they paid and when as text (`claim_note`);
  the statement match is the proof. Images return with the WhatsApp media phase.
- **Statement files are parsed on upload and never stored.** The gateway parses the CSV (bounded: 5 MB, 20,000 lines)
  into `statement_lines` inside the request and discards the file, so nothing sits in the versioned bucket. Matching
  runs in a queue-driven job (like the knowledge-base ingest), not in the request. `description` keeps the text
  needed for the reference and payer-name match with every digit run of nine or more characters masked, except the
  invoice reference; lines are purged 90 days after import by a retention job (match results stay).
- **References and account numbers avoid the redaction and grounding traps.** A reference is 4 to 6 characters of
  letters and digits from an unambiguous alphabet plus one check character (for example `K7QX-4M`), never a long
  digit run, so the phone and card masking and the numeric-value grounding check cannot mangle it. **Payment
  instructions are a deterministic reply built from facts** (as `booking_write_facts` does), not model text: account
  number, reference and amount are inserted exactly; the stored memory copy may be masked, and the agent reads the
  facts again when asked.
- **Invoices.** Customer-facing reads show only `issued`, `overdue`, `proof_submitted`, `cash_pending` and `paid`
  invoices, never `draft` or `void`. `invoices.id` stays a globally unique text (`inv_` plus a time-ordered
  random id); the company-unique `reference` is a separate column. The `download_link` action of the invoice agent
  is removed in Payments 1: it promises a link whose handler does not exist, so invoices are shown as text facts
  until a PDF is built.
- **Periods and prices.** Periods keep rolling as today (`subscriptions_due_renew` is unchanged and does not look at
  payment). The generator makes the invoice for the period that starts at `renews_at` seven days ahead, and uses the
  plan in effect at that period's start, so a scheduled plan change (`pending_plan`) prices the first period it
  covers. A `pending` subscription's period starts at its first payment. The suspend rule (off by default) sets the
  subscription to `paused` with reason `unpaid` and resumes it on payment; no new status.
- **Cash through Bookings.** `choose_cash` sets the invoice to `cash_pending` and replies with a deterministic
  message offering to book a meeting; the next turn plans the existing booking spoke as for any booking. A booking
  type can be flagged `collects_payment` (new column) and a booking can carry `invoice_id` (new nullable column);
  cash is offered only when the company has the Bookings package and such a type. Cancelling the booking or a
  no-show returns the invoice to `issued`; the staff **Collect cash** action on the booking writes the payment and
  marks it paid.
- **Package.** The `payment_agent` capability and the Payments tab belong to the existing `subscriptions` package (no
  new sellable package); the jobs use the same package gate as `csa/jobs/subscriptions.py`.
- **Open for the owner (section 10).** Reminder channel for WhatsApp-only customers; bank statement format.

## 10. Owner decisions raised by the builder check

Decided 2026-11-11: the owner asked for these to be resolved appropriately, so both defaults below stand.

1. **WhatsApp-only customers and reminders.** A job cannot message a WhatsApp customer outside the 24-hour window
   without an approved template, and Telegram is the only proactive channel today. Default: the sign-up asks for an
   optional email and reminders fall back to email (the booking reminders already do), the WhatsApp template phase
   is scheduled as its own track (it needs Meta approval), and until then WhatsApp customers with no email appear
   in a short "Needs a nudge" list for staff.
2. **Statement format.** Default: CSV export with a saved column mapping per company (Maybank, CIMB and Public Bank
   business accounts normally export CSV); a PDF-only bank is a later add.

## 11. Builder check 3 (2026-11-13): the Payments 1 plan

The build (one pull request, fourteen tasks, migrations 0590 and 0591, plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-13-payments-1.md`)
departs from sections 2 to 10 in the places below. Each is the design for P1 now; where a sentence above says
otherwise, this section wins. Each ruling says what it costs if it was the wrong call. Payments 2 (statement import,
cash through Bookings) is not part of it.

### 11.1 Deviations from the design and the survey

1. **Payments are off until an Admin switches them on** (`payment_settings.enabled`, which needs one active bank or
   DuitNow method). The design has the generator run for every active subscription; on rollout that would invoice and
   message every customer of every company with the Subscriptions package, including companies that collect money
   another way. *If wrong:* one extra switch per company before invoices start.
2. **No QR image in P1.** The `qr` method carries the DuitNow ID and name as text; there is no outbound image
   anywhere (Telegram send is text, WhatsApp is plain text), and adding it is media and storage work. *If wrong:*
   customers who prefer to scan must type the DuitNow ID, which any Malaysian bank app accepts (see 11.3).
3. **Every payment answer is a facts-only reply**, not just the instructions: status and claim replies are the facts as
   written in English or Malay (`AgentContext.locale` from the message language), so the bot can never say "received"
   without a `payments` row. A turn that asks for payment information replies with the payment facts, preceded by what
   the same turn's other tasks wrote and committed (a booking, a ticket, a sign-up request, an order draft), stated
   first; that turn's other reads are dropped and no model is called. Facts that can now be sent word for word (the
   sign-up read-back, phone invalid, try later, the ticket cancel question, the draft order) were reworded to speak to
   the customer. *If wrong:* stiffer wording, and a read asked together with a payment question needs a second message.
4. **Cash through Bookings is wholly Payments 2** (`choose_cash`, `booking_types.collects_payment`,
   `bookings.invoice_id`, Collect cash), as the programme split says; P1 keeps the `cash` method kind, the
   `cash_pending` status and `source 'cash'` so P2 adds no rewrite. Meanwhile staff record cash with Mark paid (method
   Cash). *If wrong:* none for P1 users.
5. **Only subscription invoices are generated.** Orders stay cash on delivery with `orders.payment_status`; the
   design's "an order becomes an invoice" waits for a later change. *If wrong:* an order paid by transfer is not
   tracked here.
6. **Reference format `XXXX-XXC` with no company prefix** (section 5 lists a prefix on the method; the reference
   belongs to the invoice, and a prefix lengthens what customers type into a 20-character bank reference field). *If
   wrong:* a prefix column can be added later without changing existing references.
7. **One payment per invoice and an append-only ledger, no undo in P1.** Short and over payments become P2 statement
   exceptions, not payments. *If wrong:* a wrong Mark paid needs the operator fix in `docs/RUNBOOK.md`, section
   "Payments".
8. **The optional sign-up email is asked only on WhatsApp and only when payments are on** (owner default 10.1 is about
   WhatsApp-only customers; Telegram is already a proactive channel). *If wrong:* a Telegram customer who leaves
   Telegram has no email fallback.
9. **Quiet hours (21:00 to 09:00), one reminder per contact per day, and the reminder language from `contacts.locale`
   are constants.** *If wrong:* a company wanting other hours needs a code change.
10. **`payment_agent` is switched on by 0591 wherever `subscription_agent` is on** and says "payment details aren't set
    up" until the company switches payments on. *If wrong:* such companies' customers get that honest answer instead
    of a team handoff.
11. **Staff "Start subscription" stays as an override.** It gives the opening invoice its first period (the new start
    to the new renewal date) and leaves it open and payable; paying it later only records the payment. Opening
    invoices are due `lead_days` after issue; a pending subscription whose customer said they paid is not expired;
    expiry and cancellation void the open invoices of the periods that will not run, the opening invoice included
    (the running period's invoice stays owed). *If wrong:* staff void an invoice by hand after a manual start.
12. **The live (contact, plan) rule is an advisory lock and a 409 in `create_subscription`, not a unique index**
    (onboarding follow-up 3): the linking service's `_UNIQUES` cannot express a status list, and a unique index would
    refuse links between two holders of one plan. *If wrong:* a link can still merge two contacts that each hold the
    plan; staff cancel one.
13. **Read-only members do not see Payments** ("Admin and staff" in the design; financial data).
14. **Survey corrections.** `invoices` matches section 2; its RLS comes from the 0110 loop and its grants from 0100;
    `GET /console/v1/invoices` returned `amount` as a float (now a decimal string); the invoice agent's download-link
    route is removed with the action; `tests/test_contact_linking_schema.py:374` pinned 0581; the onboarding design's
    "approve creates the first invoice" is now true only with payments on; the golden runner seeds Telegram chats
    only, so WhatsApp-only behaviour (the sign-up email, reminders) is covered by unit and job tests, not golden
    cases.

### 11.2 Rulings made during the build

These came out of the per-task reviews and are in the code and tests. Each costs a small change if the owner
disagrees.

1. **Check character.** The reference's check character is a weighted mod-31 sum (weights 2 and 1, the doubled value
   counted as 2x mod 31), not the textbook Luhn fold, which cannot catch every single wrong character in base 31. It
   catches every single wrong character and every adjacent swap. Nothing in the database depends on it beyond the
   pattern.
2. **A cancelled subscription's open invoice can still be marked paid, and only the payment is recorded.** Money that
   arrived must be recordable. The subscription is not touched (no activation, no resume, no new dates; a
   `payment_recorded` event with `recorded_only` goes on its timeline). Nothing is un-cancelled or un-voided; staff
   re-subscribe the customer by hand if they come back.
3. **A subscription is suspended for non-payment at most once per invoice.** If staff resume it while the invoice
   stays overdue, the next run does not pause it again for that invoice (a later overdue invoice still can).
4. **Paused-unpaid subscriptions are not renewed while paused.** Paying lifts the pause and gives a fresh period from
   the payment day; the periods that would have run during the pause are never invoiced.
5. **A period is invoiced only if it starts within `lead_days`** (and after now). A worker outage longer than
   `lead_days` across a renewal means that period is never invoiced; the same rule stops switching payments on from
   billing a period that is already running. Follow-up: a staff-visible "unbilled periods" check.
6. **Zero-price subscriptions get no invoice** (a payment must be more than zero), and a free sign-up that staff
   approve gets the wording without an invoice.
7. **Reminder email is built at send time** with the day's date, has its own closing line (an email reply goes to a
   sender nobody reads, so it does not say "reply here"), is dropped after 48 hours (`STALE_AFTER`, never sent late)
   and waits for 09:00 in quiet hours. The Replies Off rule that stops booking reminder mail also stops payment
   reminder mail (`customers_remindable` is false for a company with Customer support).
8. **The planner's `escalate` for a disputed or double charge now survives plan validation** (`plan_rules.py`) for
   companies with `payment_agent`; before, an account intent with no task was turned into a clarifying question.
9. **The sign-up email is asked after the phone** and its ask discloses where the address is kept: "We keep it on your
   contact record to send you messages about your plan and bookings." This is a deliberate departure from the plan's
   copy, because booking mail also uses the address. The shared sign-up data note ("We keep your name and phone number
   ...") was not changed (owner decision, still open in `docs/CUSTOMER_BOT_RETEST.md`).
10. **Payments settings are an in-page view** (`/payments?view=settings`, `/portal/payments?view=settings`), visible to
    Admin and staff, read-only for staff; there are no separate routes.
11. **The invoice agent lost its download link**, so `INVOICE_SIGNING_SECRET` is now unused (a chore to remove it).
