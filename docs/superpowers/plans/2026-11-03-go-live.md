# Go live Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A tenant admin sets the brand name and turns customer replies on from the Services page, and nobody can be silent without knowing.

**Architecture:** A small `csa/console/customer_replies.py` holds the read, the save and two routes under `/console/v1/services`. `activate_package` moves a new company to `pilot`. The web gets one panel beside `WhatsAppPanel` and one shared banner on Overview and Inbox.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.12, FastAPI, psycopg3, Postgres 16 testcontainers, Next.js with node:test, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-go-live-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Modes on the wire: `off`, `approve`, `auto`, mapping to `shadow`, `suggest`, `auto`.
- Warning codes: `replies_off`, `no_knowledge`, `base_only`.
- Refusals: 422 `brand_name_required`, 409 `package_suspended`.
- Audit actions: `tenant.status_changed`, `tenant.customer_replies_set`.
- Copy, exactly:
  - "The name your bot uses with customers"
  - "Off: customers get no reply"

  - "Your bot has nothing to answer from yet. Add files in Knowledge base."
  - "With Customer support alone, most customer questions go to your team."
  - "Your customers aren't getting replies yet."
  - "Ask your tenant admin to turn replies on."

## Review Focus

1. **A brand name with newlines, control characters or only spaces.** It goes into the bot's prompt. It's refused, or trimmed for spaces, and never stored with a line break. (Task 1)
2. **A company whose stored mode and policy disagree, or that has no policy set.** The gate reads `config.policy` and treats a missing one as `auto`, so the panel must report what the bot actually does, not the column. (Task 1)
3. **A company already `active`, or `suspended`.** Saving replies or activating Customer support leaves those statuses alone; only `onboarding` moves. (Task 1)
4. **Turning replies off with no brand name, and saving with no change.** Off always works. A repeat writes no audit row. (Task 1)
5. **The banner for a member with no Customer support, a platform admin, or a failed read.** It shows nothing, and the page works. (Task 2)
