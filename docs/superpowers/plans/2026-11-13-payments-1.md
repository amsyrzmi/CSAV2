# Payments 1: Invoices, Payment Methods, Reminders, the Bot's Payment Answers and the Payments Tab (P1) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Once a company switches payments on, every subscription period and every approved sign-up gets an invoice with a short checksummed reference, the customer is reminded on Telegram, in an open WhatsApp chat or by email, the bot gives exact payment instructions and honest status from facts, staff mark invoices paid in a Payments tab (which starts a pending subscription), and the ledger is the only truth for "paid".

**Architecture:** One pull request, migrations `0590` (payments schema) and `0591` (the `payment_agent` capability and the sign-up email). A new package `csa/payments/` holds the reference codec, settings and methods, the invoice ledger service (issue, mark paid, void, claim) and the copy; `csa/jobs/payments.py` generates invoices and marks overdue, `csa/jobs/payment_reminders.py` queues reminders and sends their mail. A new `PaymentAgent` (`csa/agents/payments/`) answers from facts and its reply is the facts themselves (no model text). The console gets `/console/v1/payments` and a Payments rail page. Payments 2 adds statement imports and cash through Bookings on the same tables: `payments.source`, `statement_line_id`, `matched_by`, method kind `cash` and status `cash_pending` exist from now on.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.12, FastAPI, psycopg 3 async with SQL in `db/sql/`, PostgreSQL migrations with RLS, the worker's job registry, Groq JSON prompts in `csa/decide/llm.py`, golden cases (`tests/golden/`), Next.js console (`CSA_WebConsole/csa-console/web/`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-payments-design.md` (sections 5, 9 and 10 bind, except where "Deviations from the spec" below says otherwise, each with its cost if wrong). Also `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-business-surfaces-programme.md` (P1) and `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-customer-onboarding-design.md` section 10 (pending subscriptions, `LINKED_TABLES`). ## Global Constraints

- Migrations `0590_payments_core.sql` and `0591_payment_agent.sql` (range 0590–0599; 0592–0599 stay for Payments 2). Additive, header with pre-check and rollback prose (pattern `0512_bookings_bot.sql:1-74`), no `FORCE` RLS, no `CREATE INDEX CONCURRENTLY`. `EXPECTED_SCHEMA_VERSION` (`apps/gateway/main.py:71`) `"0581"` → `"0591"`; `tests/test_contact_linking_schema.py:374` drops `== MIGRATION`; add both files to `db/migrations/README.md` and a "Roll back migration 0590/0591" paragraph each to `docs/RUNBOOK.md`.
- **Money:** amounts are `numeric(12,2)` with a `currency` (3 upper-case letters) in SQL and `Decimal` in Python and JSON (serialised as a string); never `float`. Text shows money with `csa.agents.facts.money` (`"MYR 1,059.90"`). The platform never holds money: no gateway, card or provider fields.
- **The ledger is the truth.** An invoice is `paid` only in the transaction that inserts its `payments` row. `payments` is append-only for `csa_app` (`SELECT, INSERT` only), has `UNIQUE (client_id, idempotency_key)` and a unique partial index `payments_one_per_invoice (client_id, invoice_id) WHERE invoice_id IS NOT NULL`, so an invoice is never paid twice. Customer-facing reads treat a `paid` invoice without a payments row (old seeds) as not shown.
- **Invoices:** `id` is `inv_` + 13 hex digits of epoch milliseconds + 12 random hex digits (`new_invoice_id`); `reference` is `XXXX-XXC`: six characters drawn from `ALPHABET = "23456789ABCDEFGHJKMNPQRSTUVWXYZ"` (31, no 0, 1, I, L, O) split 4 + 2 by a dash, plus one Luhn mod 31 check character; DB check `^[2-9A-HJKMNP-Z]{4}-[2-9A-HJKMNP-Z]{3}$`; unique per company (`invoices_reference_uq`). One invoice per subscription period, void ones included: `invoices_subscription_period_uq (client_id, subscription_id, period_start) NULLS NOT DISTINCT WHERE subscription_id IS NOT NULL`; the opening invoice of a pending subscription has `period_start` NULL until it is paid. Status list: `draft, issued, proof_submitted, paid, overdue, void, cash_pending`; customers see only `issued, overdue, proof_submitted, cash_pending, paid`.
- **Nothing happens until a company switches payments on** (`payment_settings.enabled`, default false, needs one active non-cash method). Off: no invoice is generated or issued at approval, no reminder, the agent answers `payment_not_set_up`. Jobs also need the active `subscriptions` package (`PACKAGE_KEYS = ["subscriptions"]`, as `csa/jobs/subscriptions.py:16-17`).
- **Reminders:** steps are days relative to `due_at` from `payment_settings.reminder_days` (default `{-7,0,3,7}`, at most 6, each −30..60); one `invoice_reminders` row per (invoice, step), `UNIQUE (client_id, invoice_id, step)`, message key `invoice:{id}:reminder:{step}`; only `issued` and `overdue` invoices with a contact; older owed steps are recorded `skipped`, never sent late; quiet hours 21:00–09:00 in `clients.timezone` (`QUIET_START`, `QUIET_END`); at most `DAILY_CAP = 1` reminder per contact per local day; channel order Telegram (when `csa.bookings.bot.gate` allows), WhatsApp only when the contact's latest WhatsApp inbound is under 23 hours old, else email (`contacts.email`, else `customer_given_email`), else a `no_channel` row that feeds "Needs a nudge". Chat reminders are written as booking reminders are (`worker/insert_message.sql`, authority `reviewed`, `worker/insert_delivery.sql`).
- **Privacy:** staff-only fields (`void_reason`, `voided_by`, `payments.note`, `payments.created_by`, reminder addresses and statuses) are never in an agent fact, a composer input, a customer message or a log line; logs carry categories and ids only. A customer reads only invoices with `contact_id = ctx.contact_id`; a typed reference narrows within those and never reveals another contact's invoice. Payment instructions are a deterministic reply built from facts: account number, reference and amount are inserted exactly.
- **Tenancy:** every new table has `client_id`, ENABLE RLS with the `tenant_isolation` policy as `0541_training_library.sql`, `REVOKE ALL ... FROM PUBLIC, csa_ro, csa_identity`, grants to `csa_app` only; child rows reference invoices by the composite key `(client_id, invoice_id)` → `invoices(client_id, id)`. New tables carry no `contact_id` (invoices already move with a link: `csa/contacts/linking.py:38-51`); the catalog test `tests/test_contact_linking.py:352-367` must stay green.
- Console routes: `/console/v1/payments`, dependencies `human_session` (from `csa.subscriptions.api`) and `package_gate("subscriptions")`. Read and staff writes (mark paid, void): platform_admin, tenant_admin, tenant_staff. Settings and methods writes: platform_admin, tenant_admin. read_only gets 403. Every write audits (`console/audit.sql`).
- Prompt rules are English with Malay examples, gated per company by `_offers(ctx, "payment_agent")` (ruling E of onboarding). Tasks marked **[prompt]** run `tests/test_golden.py` in full and `tests/test_llm_provider.py`, and the staging replay after rollout.

## Review Focus

- Staff press Mark paid twice, or two staff press it at once: one payment row, the same answer for the same `request_key`, 409 `invoice_already_paid` for another key, the pending subscription activated once (Task 3 concurrency test).
- A company switches payments on with hundreds of active subscriptions: only periods starting within `lead_days` are invoiced, no past period, the job stops at its batch and the next run continues, nothing is sent in quiet hours, one reminder per contact per day (Tasks 5 and 6).
- A WhatsApp-only customer with no email and a closed 24-hour window: no message is attempted, one `no_channel` row per step, the invoice shows once in "Needs a nudge" (Task 6).
- "Did you get my payment?" before any Mark paid, after a claim, and with another customer's reference typed: never "paid" without a payments row, "not confirmed" after a claim, "can't find" for the foreign reference with nothing of it shown (Tasks 9 and 10).
- A subscription is cancelled, expires or is cancelled at period end with an invoice open: the invoice is voided and reminders stop; a pending subscription whose customer said they paid is not expired; a plan price change after issue leaves the issued amount as it was (Task 4).

## Deviations from the spec

Each with what it costs if it is the wrong call:

1. **Payments are off until an Admin switches them on** (`payment_settings.enabled`, needs one active bank or DuitNow method). The spec has the generator run for every active subscription; on rollout that would invoice and message every customer of every company with the Subscriptions package, including companies that collect money another way. *If wrong:* one extra switch per company before invoices start.
2. **No QR image in P1.** The `qr` method carries the DuitNow ID and name as text; there is no outbound image anywhere (Telegram send is text, WhatsApp is plain text), and adding it is media and storage work. *If wrong:* customers who prefer to scan must type the DuitNow ID, which any Malaysian bank app accepts (see Open questions).
3. **Every payment answer is a facts-only reply**, not just the instructions: status and claim replies are the facts as written in English or Malay (`AgentContext.locale` from the message language), so the bot can never say "received" without a payments row. A turn that also asked something else answers only the payment part. *If wrong:* stiffer wording and a mixed question needs a second message.
4. **Cash through Bookings is wholly Payments 2** (`choose_cash`, `booking_types.collects_payment`, `bookings.invoice_id`, Collect cash), as the programme split says; P1 keeps the `cash` method kind, the `cash_pending` status and `source 'cash'` so P2 adds no rewrite. Meanwhile staff record cash with Mark paid (method Cash). *If wrong:* none for P1 users.
5. **Only subscription invoices are generated.** Orders stay cash on delivery with `orders.payment_status`; the spec's "an order becomes an invoice" waits for a later change. *If wrong:* an order paid by transfer is not tracked here.
6. **Reference format `XXXX-XXC` with no company prefix** (spec section 5 lists a prefix on the method; the reference belongs to the invoice, and a prefix lengthens what customers type into a 20-character bank reference field). *If wrong:* a prefix column can be added later without changing existing references.
7. **One payment per invoice and an append-only ledger, no undo in P1.** Short and over payments become P2 statement exceptions, not payments. *If wrong:* a wrong Mark paid needs the operator fix in the RUNBOOK.
8. **The optional sign-up email is asked only on WhatsApp and only when payments are on** (owner default 10.1 is about WhatsApp-only customers; Telegram is already a proactive channel). *If wrong:* a Telegram customer who leaves Telegram has no email fallback.
9. **Quiet hours (21:00–09:00), one reminder per contact per day, and the reminder language from `contacts.locale` are constants.** *If wrong:* a company wanting other hours needs a code change.
10. **`payment_agent` is switched on by 0591 wherever `subscription_agent` is on** and says "payment details aren't set up" until the company switches payments on. *If wrong:* such companies' customers get that honest answer instead of a team handoff.
11. **Staff "Start subscription" stays as an override** and leaves the opening invoice open; opening invoices are due `lead_days` after issue; a pending subscription whose customer said they paid is not expired; expiry and cancellation void the open invoices of the periods that will not run. *If wrong:* staff void an opening invoice by hand after a manual start.
12. **The live (contact, plan) rule is an advisory lock and a 409 in `create_subscription`, not a unique index** (PROGRESS follow-up 3): the linking service's `_UNIQUES` cannot express a status list, and a unique index would refuse links between two holders of one plan. *If wrong:* a link can still merge two contacts that each hold the plan; staff cancel one.
13. **Read-only members do not see Payments** ("Admin and staff" in the spec; financial data).
14. **Survey corrections:** `invoices` matches spec section 2; its RLS comes from the 0110 loop and its grants from 0100; `GET /console/v1/invoices` returned `amount` as a float (now a decimal string); the invoice agent's download-link route is removed with the action; `tests/test_contact_linking_schema.py:374` pinned 0581; the onboarding spec's "approve creates the first invoice" is now true only with payments on; the golden runner seeds Telegram chats only, so WhatsApp-only behaviour (the sign-up email, reminders) is covered by unit and job tests, not golden cases.
