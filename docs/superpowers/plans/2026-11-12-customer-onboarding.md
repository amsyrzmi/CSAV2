# Customer Onboarding: Sign-up From the Chat and One Customer Across Channels (PR 2b) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A customer signs up in the chat (the bot collects name, phone and plan, reads them back, files one request on a typed "yes"); staff approve or decline in a Sign-ups section or a plan approves automatically; an approved sign-up is a `pending` subscription that staff activate or that expires. The same person on Telegram and WhatsApp becomes one customer when the channel verified their phone; staff can link and unlink by hand.

**Architecture:** One pull request, two migrations. Sign-up: new `signup_requests` table and service `csa/subscriptions/signup.py`; `SubscriptionAgent.request_signup` confirms by a typed yes after a read-back (works on both channels, no buttons). Linking: a service `csa/contacts/linking.py` that, at link time, **re-points every contact-owned row to the surviving contact** (conversations, orders, subscriptions, bookings, invoices and the rest) and the channel identities, so ingress, the inbox and the bot need no `merged_into` followers; the losing contact stays as an empty shell with `merged_into` for audit and unlink. Phone verification is one SQL normaliser behind a generated column and a unique partial index; a Python mirror is tested equal to it.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.12, FastAPI, psycopg 3 async with SQL under `db/sql/`, PostgreSQL migrations with RLS, Groq JSON calls in `csa/decide/llm.py`, golden cases (`tests/golden/`), Next.js console (`CSA_WebConsole/csa-console/web/`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-customer-onboarding-design.md` (approved; sections 5 and 9 bind except where Deviations below overrides them, each with its cost if wrong). Also `2026-11-10-business-surfaces-programme.md` (PR 2b) and `2026-11-11-payments-design.md` (what activation waits on later). Facts are from a read-only survey of main 2f4df4c (inlined where it matters).

## Review Focus

- A customer types a phone equal to another customer's: the sign-up files with the typed number, nothing of the other contact is read, shown or linked (Task 8).
- A Telegram user forwards someone else's contact card or shares one with a different `user_id`: refused with the plain sentence, nothing stored, no link (Task 7).
- Two contacts hold the same phone digits, or a Telegram account is already on the survivor: no link, both untouched, a review row appears once (Tasks 2 and 6).
- After a link, the next Telegram and WhatsApp messages from both people land in one conversation thread on the survivor with no 409, the bot sees the subscription from either channel, and unlink puts every row back (Tasks 2, 6, 7).
- A link racing with an incoming message on the other channel: one outcome, no lost or duplicated rows (advisory lock on both ids; Task 2 concurrency test).
- "Yes" to something that was not a read-back files nothing; a repeat or a plan already held files nothing (Task 8).
- Staff approve a request whose plan was archived, closed or repriced, or approve twice at once: 409 and one subscription at most; a pending subscription is never "renews on", cannot be paused, can be activated or cancelled, and expires after the company's days (Tasks 3, 4, 9).
- Malay: asking, read-back and "received" keep the casual register; a bare "ya" confirms only right after a read-back (Task 8).

## Deviations from the spec / survey corrections

Rulings, each with what it costs if wrong:

1. **Linking re-points all contact-owned rows instead of adding `merged_into` followers.** The design (section 9) re-points only subscriptions, bookings and invoices and makes the console and inbox follow `merged_into`. The survey found about thirty readers, and the Telegram ingress returns 409 when a conversation's contact differs from the resolved contact, so that design breaks the next message after a link. Re-pointing conversations, orders, tickets and the rest to the survivor keeps every reader and the ingress unchanged. *If wrong:* a bigger link transaction and a heavier unlink (ids per table in `moved`); an unlink after new activity leaves the new rows with the survivor.
2. **Staff-entered phones count as a match, typed phones never.** The Telegram share links to exactly one contact with the same digits whether that contact's number was verified or staff-entered (the WhatsApp resolver already trusts staff numbers). *If wrong:* a mistyped staff number links two people; staff can unlink within 30 days.
3. **Unlink after 30 days is refused (409), not offered "by hand".** The design says by hand only; no safe manual path exists once rows have moved. *If wrong:* an old wrong link can only be fixed by linking the other way.
4. **Typed read-back and typed "yes" replaces the confirm button.** Neither a confirm button for this agent nor a typed fallback on WhatsApp exists (`UiConfirmation.order_id` is required; WhatsApp ignores `ui`); the booking cancel already confirms by a typed yes (`booking.cancel_asked`, `confirmed true`). *If wrong:* a model that mistakes "ok" for a yes files an unwanted request; staff approval is the default and pending never charges.
5. **Staff activate until payments exist.** Nothing creates subscription invoices yet; P1 will. An auto-approve plan creates a pending subscription staff still start, or it expires after the company's days. *If wrong:* auto-approve saves less staff time than designed until P1.
6. **Archiving a plan stays unblocked.** `plan_in_use` runs only when the interval changes; blocking archives would also change active and paused plans. `is_signup_open` is how a company stops sign-ups. Pending counts toward the interval-change block only.
7. **No staff push notification for a new sign-up in this PR.** The tab shows an open count; if filing a pause or cancel request notifies staff elsewhere, mirror it as a follow-up.
8. **The "Link contacts" anchor is the subscription drawer plus a "Needs review" section in the Sign-ups tab**, because the Customers view lists subscriptions, not contacts, and a contact with no subscription never appears there.
9. **A typed sign-up phone is never copied to `contacts.phone`** (found by the Task 2 review: the copy plus trusting staff-entered phones lets a typed number link its owner's contact to the typer). *If wrong:* no route lets staff edit `contacts.phone`; staff link the two contacts by hand instead.
10. **Survey corrections to the design:** `renews_at > started_at` is a `WHERE` in `subscription_insert.sql`, not a table check; the portal "twin" is the same `SubscriptionsWorkspace`; PR 2's `mine` read already handles `pending`; no subscription settings table existed; golden cases cannot send a callback tap or a contact share (those are covered in `test_ingress.py`, `test_telegram.py`, `test_delivery_buttons.py`, `test_staged_agents.py`); `contact_verifications` is unused and unsuitable and stays untouched; the baseline `subscription_requests` table is unused and left alone; a phone normaliser needs an IMMUTABLE SQL function behind a generated column because the unique index cannot call Python.
